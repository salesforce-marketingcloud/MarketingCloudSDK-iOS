InboxResponseDelegate

@objc(SFInboxResponseDelegate)
public protocol InboxResponseDelegate

Receives successful inbox ingest results from an operational producer (MCE or MAM). Failures do not invoke this protocol; use the refreshMessages completion for refresh outcomes.
